Account & Data Deletion Request

Edu Champ Junior

If you would like to request deletion of your Edu Champ Junior account and associated personal information, or if you would like to request that your learning and performance data be reset, you can submit a request directly through the Edu Champ Junior App.

Important: Please use the in-app contact/support feature to submit your request. Including your Membership Key helps our support team identify the correct account and process your request efficiently.

1. Request Account and Data Deletion

If you want your Edu Champ Junior account and associated personal information to be deleted, please follow these steps:

  1. Open the Edu Champ Junior App.
  2. Open the Contact / Support section in the App.
  3. Select or mention that you are requesting account and data deletion.
  4. Include your Membership Key in the request.
  5. Submit the request to our support team.

Our support team will review the request and take the appropriate steps to process the deletion.

2. Request Performance Data Reset

If you do not want to delete your account but would like to start your learning performance history again, you may request a reset of your performance data.

To request a performance data reset:

  1. Open the Edu Champ Junior App.
  2. Open the Contact / Support section.
  3. Clearly state that you are requesting a performance data reset.
  4. Include your Membership Key.
  5. Submit the request to our support team.
Please note: A performance data reset may permanently remove or reset learning performance information associated with your account. Please make this request only if you are sure you want your performance history to be reset.

3. What Happens After You Submit a Request?

Requests are handled by the Edu Champ Junior support team. The team may verify the Membership Key and other account information before processing the request to help protect the account from unauthorized requests.

Once the request has been reviewed, the support team will process the applicable action and may contact you through the available contact method if additional information or verification is required.

4. What Can Be Requested?

Account and Personal Data Deletion

Request deletion of your Edu Champ Junior account and associated personal information, subject to any information that we may be required to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate purposes.

Performance Data Reset

Request the reset of learning and performance data associated with your account without necessarily deleting the account itself.

5. Need Help?

If you have questions about account deletion, performance data, or any other privacy-related request, please contact our support team through the in-app Contact / Support feature.
